What this shows

These were solvency and peg failures, not slow drains of liquidity. Right up to the end, trading volume looked healthy, so a model built on volume and volatility rated them highly. The grades collapsed after the event. That's when the exit really did vanish, and the model measured it within days. The honest conclusion: Exit measures how costly leaving is. It is not a crash predictor.
